# Customizing Pious

Pious can look the way you want, and change how Roblox looks and sounds,
without anything being baked in: **themes** change Pious, **plugins** can
bring themes, icons, styles, sounds and Roblox files, and **Tweaks** apply
Roblox mods.

## How Pious looks (Settings → Appearance)

- **Theme:** pick one to use its whole look. You can still adjust anything
  after; **Reset** goes back to Pious's own look.
- **Colors:** accent, second accent, background, glass and text.
- **Gradient:** a gradient across the background, its two colors, angle
  and strength.
- **Font:** Pious's own (Manrope) or any font installed on your PC, with a
  preview.
- **Roundness** and **text size**.
- **Background picture**, dimmed and blurred as you like.
- **See-through** window with Windows' blur, a stronger blur, or none.
- **Interface size**, and **reduce motion**.

## Themes

A theme is a folder with a `theme.json` (and any pictures, fonts, icons or
a stylesheet it uses). Put it in the `themes` folder (Settings →
Appearance → **Themes folder**) and pick it. Plugins can bring themes too;
those appear while the plugin is on. How to make one: **Making a theme**.

## Roblox: cursors, sounds, death sounds and more

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Classic cursors, old sounds, old avatar background, emoji style, font, Roblox icon, window title, shift lock cursor | Tweaks → Mods |
| Death sound, cursor, shift lock cursor, any sound or texture, as an installable pack | A plugin (see Plugins) |
| Any Roblox file you want to replace by hand | Your mods folder (Tweaks → Mods → Mods folder) |

Plugins' Roblox files apply while the plugin and Tweaks are on, after
Pious's own mods; your mods folder wins over everything. All of it is undone
when Tweaks are turned off.

## Pious's own icons, styles and sounds

Plugins and themes can replace Pious's icons (by name), add a stylesheet
that restyles any part of Pious, and replace the notification sound. They
can't run code in Pious's window: icons are shown as pictures and styles
are only styles.
